# OIBSIP-GRAPHICDESIGN-TASK5-MAGAZINE-BROCHURE
Skyline Real Estate - Trifold Brochure Mockup
This repository contains the layout and content assets for the official marketing trifold brochure of Skyline Real Estate. The design showcases a comprehensive view of both the inner spreads and outer flaps of the print asset.  
🏠 Brand & Project Profile
Company Name: Skyline Real Estate  
Core Value Statement: "Discover modern homes designed for your comfort, happiness, and lifestyle."  
Tagline: Building Dreams, Creating Futures
📖 Brochure Structure & Content Breakdown
1. External Panel / Cover Layout (Page 1)
The exterior layout establishes the primary brand image with high-contrast architectural photography and bold value propositions.  
Front Cover Panel:  
Logo: Minimalist golden line-art house icon.  
Headlines: SKYLINE REAL ESTATE / YOUR DREAM HOME AWAITS.  
Subheadline: "Discover modern homes designed for your comfort, happiness, and lifestyle."  
Main Spread Panels (Promotional Highlights):  
Displays a premium modern two-story luxury residence with exterior lighting.  
Feature Highlights (Iconography Perks):  
Spacious and elegant interiors  
Premium facilities  
Safe and peaceful surroundings
2. Internal Panels / Information Spread (Page 2)
The inner layout uses a clean white and vibrant cyan grid with structured sections to break down specific offerings.  
Panel A: "Step Into Your New House"  
Focuses on property features paired with a modern residential building image:  
Spacious Living  
Family-friendly Homes  
Premium Interiors  
Convenient Locations  
Panel B: "Why Choose Us?"
A dedicated block for company positioning:  
"At Skyline Real Estate, we help you find a home that matches your dreams and lifestyle. Explore thoughtfully designed properties with modern amenities, quality construction, and comfortable living spaces"
Panel C: "Book Now" / Call to Action (CTA)  
Features luxury kitchen and interior living space photography along with contact details:  
📞 Phone: +91 9876543210  
✉️ Email: info@skylinerealestate.com
🌐 Website: www.skylinerealestate.com
📍 Location: Siliguri, India
🎨 Design & Print Specifications
Palette:
Primary: Vibrant Cyan / Blue (#00A8C6 approx.)
Accent: Gold / Yellow line accents
Neutral Backgrounds: Crisp White and Soft Grey
Typography: Modern serif headlines combined with clean geometric sans-serif body copy.
Layout Style: Trifold template (Standard 8.5" x 11" or A4 equivalent dimension scale).
